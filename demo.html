How long is the demo session?
The demo session is 1 hour long. It includes a live interactive session where you can experience our teaching methodology, interact with expert trainers, get an overview of the course curriculum, and understand the learning path and career opportunities.
Is the demo completely free?
Yes, absolutely! The demo class is 100% free with no hidden charges. There is no obligation to enroll after the demo. It's designed to help you understand our training approach, evaluate the quality of our instructors, and make an informed decision before committing.
Can I ask questions during the demo?
Yes, definitely! The demo is interactive and we encourage questions. You can ask about course content, career opportunities, placement assistance, certification process, and any other doubts you may have. Our trainers will be happy to address all your queries.
What happens after the demo?
After the demo, you'll receive a follow-up call/email from our team. We'll provide detailed course information and fee structure. You can clarify any remaining doubts. If you're interested, you can proceed with enrollment. There is no pressure to enroll immediately – we respect your decision.

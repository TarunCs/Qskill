Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our programs.

What is the duration of the courses?
Our courses typically range from 2 to 6 months, depending on the program and schedule you choose. We offer both weekend and weekday batches to accommodate working professionals.
Do you offer job placement assistance?
Yes! We have a dedicated placement team that provides resume building, interview preparation, and connects you with our hiring partners. Our placement assistance continues until you land your dream job.
What are the prerequisites for the courses?
Basic understanding of software development and programming concepts is helpful but not mandatory. We have courses suitable for beginners as well as experienced professionals.
Do you provide certification upon completion?
Yes, you will receive an industry-recognized certification upon successful completion of the course. Our certifications are valued by top companies in the software testing domain.
Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?
Absolutely! We offer free demo classes so you can experience our teaching methodology and interact with our trainers before making a decision.
